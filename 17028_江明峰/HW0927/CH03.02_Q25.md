$Sx = \sqrt{\frac{\sum(x_i - \bar{x})^2}{n - 1}}=4.141926551$

$Sy = \sqrt{\frac{\sum(y_i - \bar{y})^2}{n - 1}}=1.8529256$  

$\bar{x}=26.6$  

$\bar{y}=16.9$
