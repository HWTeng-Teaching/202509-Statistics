### c.
$\bar x = \frac{266}{10}=26.6$  
  
$\bar y = \frac{169}{10}=16.9$

$S_x=\sqrt{17.16}=4.14$

$S_y=\sqrt{3.43}=1.85$

$S_{xy} =\frac{\sum x_iy_i-\frac{(\sum x_i)(\sum y_i)}{n}}{n-1} =\frac{4558-\frac{266 \times 169}{10}}{10-1}=6.96$

$r=\frac{S_{xy}}{S_xS_y}=\frac{6.96}{4.14 \times 1.85}=0.91$

$b=r\frac{S_y}{S_x}=0.91 \cdot \frac{1.85}{4.14}=0.41$

$a=\bar y - b \bar x=16.9-0.41 \times 26.6=5.99$  
  
The least-squares line: $y=5.99+0.41x$

### d.
$y=5.99+0.41 \times 27=17.06$  
≈17 chirps/second.

### b.
$S_x=\sqrt{90.1}=9.49$

  
$S_y=\sqrt{1910.2}=43.71$  
  
$S_{xy}  =\frac{290252-\frac{1378 \times 5680}{28}}{28-1}=\frac{10714.86}{27}=396.85$  
  
$r=\frac{S_{xy}}{S_xS_y}=\frac{396.85}{9.49 \times 43.71}=0.96$  , very strong, positive linear relationship

### d.
$\bar x = \frac{1378}{28}=49.21$  
  
$\bar y = \frac{5680}{28}=202.86$  

$b=r\frac{S_y}{S_x}=0.96 \cdot \frac{43.71}{9.49}=4.42$  
  
$a=\bar y - b \bar x=202.86-4.42 \times 49.21=-14.65$  
  
$y=-14.65+4.42x$  
  
$y=-14.65+4.42 \times 48=197.51$
