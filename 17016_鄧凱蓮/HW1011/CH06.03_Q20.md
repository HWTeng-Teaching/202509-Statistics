## Ans:

### a.If the 50,000 children were a random sample from the population of children represented by past records, what is the probability of observing a value of x equal to 60 or more?

總檢查人數 ($n$) $= 50,000$

基因缺陷的機率 ($p$) $= 1/1000 = 0.001$

觀察到的缺陷兒童數 ($x$) $= 60$

平均數 (μ) =np = $\mu = 50,000 \cdot 0.001 = 50$

變異數 $(\sigma^2) = n \cdot p \cdot (1-p) = 50,000 \cdot 0.001 \cdot (1 - 0.001) = 50 \cdot 0.999 = 49.95$

標準差 $(\sigma) = \sqrt{\sigma^2} = \sqrt{49.95} \approx 7.0675$

$P(X \ge 60) \approx P(X} \ge 60 - 0.5) = P(X \ge 59.5)$ 連續修正

$z = \frac{X - \mu}{\sigma} = \frac{59.5 - 50}{7.0675} = \frac{9.5}{7.0675} \approx 1.34$

$P(Z \ge 1.34) = 1 - P(Z < 1.34) = 1 - 0.9099$

$P(X \ge 60) \approx 0.0901$

### b. Would you say that the observation of x=60 children with genetic defects represents a rare event?

$P(X \ge 60) \approx 0.0901$。

由於 $0.0901 > 0.05$，從機率的標準來看，這不被視為一個罕見事件。
