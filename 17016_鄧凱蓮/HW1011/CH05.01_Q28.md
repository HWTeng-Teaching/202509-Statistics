### b.  Let x equal the number of keys that you try before you find the one that opens the door (x=1,2,3,4).Then assign the appropriate value of x to each simple event.

| x 的值 | 描述（正確鑰匙在第 k 位） | P(X) |
|:------:|:--------------------------|:-----:|
| x = 1 | 第一把為正確鑰匙 | $\=frac{1}{4}$ |
| x = 2 | 第二把為正確鑰匙 | \( = \frac{3}{4} \times \frac{1}{3} = \frac{1}{4} \) |
| x = 3 | 第三把為正確鑰匙 | \( = \frac{3}{4} \times \frac{2}{3} \times \frac{1}{2} = \frac{1}{4} \) |
| x = 4 | 第四把為正確鑰匙 | \( = \frac{3}{4} \times \frac{2}{3} \times \frac{1}{2} \times 1 = \frac{1}{4} \) |
