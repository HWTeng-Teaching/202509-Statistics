#### Ans:

#### (a)

n=50  , x̄ =8 , s=0.3

MOE= $\pm {1.96} * \dfrac{s}{\sqrt{n}}=\pm {1.96} * \dfrac{0.3}{\sqrt{50}} =\pm {0.0832}$

95% margin of error:

CI =8 $\pm {0.0832}$ =[7.9168 , 8.0832]

#### (b)

n=50  , x̄ =67.3 , s=0.64

MOE= $\pm {2.58} * \dfrac{s}{\sqrt{n}}=\pm {2.58} * \dfrac{0.64}{\sqrt{50}} =\pm {0.2335}$

99% margin of error:

CI =67.3 $\pm {0.2335}$ =[67.0665 , 67.5335]
