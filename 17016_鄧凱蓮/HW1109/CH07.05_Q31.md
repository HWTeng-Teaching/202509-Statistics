#### Ans:

__P=0.47 , n=100__ 

#### (a)

np = 100 × 0.47 = 47 ≥ 5
n(1 − p) = 100 × 0.53 = 53 ≥ 5

→ 滿足條件，可用常態分布近似。

Mean of $\hat{p}$ = 0.47

$SE ={\sqrt{\frac{pq}{n}}}$
    $={\sqrt{\frac{ 0.47 X 0.53}{100}}}$
    $=0.0499$

#### (b)

$P(\hat{P} < 0.45 )$ 

$Z = \frac{0.45-0.47}{0.0499}$ = -0.04

$P(\hat{P} < 0.45 )$ 
=P( Z < -0.04 ) , 查表得到:
=0.3446

#### (c)
$P( 0.42 <\hat{P} < 0.45 )$ 

$Z_1 = \frac{0.42-0.47}{0.0499}=-1$

$Z_2 = \frac{0.45-0.47}{0.0499}=-0.4$ 

$P( 0.42 <\hat{P} < 0.45 )$ 

$=P( -1 < Z < -0.4 )$ 

$=P(Z<-0.4)-P(Z<-1), 查表得到:$

$=0.3446-0.1587$ $=0.1859$
