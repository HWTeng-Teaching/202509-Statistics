$S_x^2 =\frac{\sum x_i^2-\frac{(\sum x_i)^2}{n}}{n-1} = \frac{70250-\frac{1378^2}{28}}{28-1}=\frac{2432.71}{27} \approx 90.1005$
  
$S_x=\sqrt{90.1005}\approx 9.4921$
  
$S_y^2 =\frac{\sum y_i^2-\frac{(\sum y_i)^2}{n}}{n-1} = \frac{1203804-\frac{5680^2}{28}}{28-1}=\frac{51575.43}{27}\approx 1,910.2011$
  
$S_y=\sqrt{1910.2011}\approx 43.7058$
  
$S_{xy} =\frac{\sum x_iy_i-\frac{(\sum x_i)(\sum y_i)}{n}}{n-1} =\frac{290252-\frac{1378 \times 5680}{28}}{28-1}=\frac{10714.86}{27}\approx 396.8466$
  
$r=\frac{S_{xy}}{S_xS_y}=\frac{396.8466}{9.4921 \times 43.7058}\approx 0.9566$

相關係數(r)為正數0.9566且接近1，表示二者具有極強的正向關係。
