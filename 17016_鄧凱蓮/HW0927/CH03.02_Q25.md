$S_x^2 =\frac{\sum x_i^2-\frac{(\sum x_i)^2}{n}}{n-1} = \frac{7230-\frac{266^2}{10}}{10-1}= \frac{154.4}{9} \approx 17.1556$
  
$S_x=\sqrt{17.1556}\approx 4.1419$
  
$S_y^2 =\frac{\sum y_i^2-\frac{(\sum y_i)^2}{n}}{n-1} = \frac{2887-\frac{169^2}{10}}{10-1} = \frac{30.9}{9} \approx 3.4333$ 
  
$S_y=\sqrt{3.4333}\approx 1.8529$
  
$S_{xy} =\frac{\sum x_iy_i-\frac{(\sum x_i)(\sum y_i)}{n}}{n-1} =\frac{4558-\frac{266 \times 169}{10}}{10-1}=\frac{62.6}{9}\approx 6.9556$
  
$r=\frac{S_{xy}}{S_xS_y}=\frac{6.9556}{4.1419 \times 1.8529}\approx 0.9063$
  
$b=r\frac{S_y}{S_x}=0.9063 \times \frac{1.8529}{4.1419}\approx 0.4054$
  
$a=\bar y - b \bar x=16.9-0.4054 \times 26.6 \approx 6.1164 $
  
The least-squares line: $y=6.6114+0.4054x$
