c.Do the data provide sufficient evidence to indicate that the average price per 1000 board meters of framing lumber differs among the four states? Test using .05 a5 .

right-side test.  

1. $H_0:\mu_1=\mu_2=\mu_3=\mu_4$  

   $H_1$:至少有一個州的平均價格與其他州不同。
   
2. &nbsp; $\alpha = 0.05$ 

3. $F_{STAT}=\frac{MST}{MSE} \sim F_({df1,df2})$  

4. &nbsp; $F^*=26.44$  

5.(1) 查表 $F_{0.05, 3, 16}$，臨界值為 3.24
  
    RR={ F：F* > 3.24 }  
    
    Since F* = 26.4 > 3.24, reject H₀.

  (2) $p-value:P(F^*|H_0)=P(F_{3,16}>26.44)<0.005$  

   $p-value <\alpha = 0.05$ $\Rightarrow Reject <\br>  H_0$
    
  6.結論: α = 0.05 的顯著水準下，有充分證據顯示這4州的平均價格不全相等。
