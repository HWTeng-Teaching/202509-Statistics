b.Do the data provide sufficient evidence to indicate a difference in mean student scores after treatment for the three methods?

right-side test.  

1. $H_0:\mu_1=\mu_2=\mu_3$  

   $H_1$:三種方法的治療後平均 HLT 分數有差異
   
2. &nbsp; $\alpha = 0.05$ 

3. $F_{STAT}=\frac{MST}{MSE} \sim F_({df1,df2})$  

4. &nbsp; $F^*=5.15$  

5.(1) 查表 $F_{0.05, 2, 8}$，臨界值為 4.46
  
    RR={ F：F* > 4.46 }  
    
    Since F* = 5.15 > 4.46, reject H₀.
    
6.結論: α = 0.05 的顯著水準下，有充分證據顯示這三種方法的平均數不全相等。
