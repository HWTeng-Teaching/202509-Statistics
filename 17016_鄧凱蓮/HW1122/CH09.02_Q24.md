__Ans:__

$\mu{0}=5.86  , &nbsp;  n=40  ,&nbsp; \bar{x}=5.56, s=0.7$


使用 左尾檢定 ( lift-sided Test)

1. &nbsp; $H_0: \mu = 5.86 &nbsp; ,&nbsp;  H_1: \mu < 5.86 $

2. &nbsp; $\alpha = 0.05$

3. &nbsp; $Z = \frac{\overline{X}_n - \mu}{s / \sqrt{n}} \xrightarrow{d} Z $

4. &nbsp; $Z^* = \frac{5.56 - 5.86}{\frac{0.7}{\sqrt{40}}} =-2.71$

5. 臨界值 (Critical Value): 對於左尾檢定， $\alpha=0.05$ 對應的 z 值為 -1.645

   RR={z:z<-1.645}
   
6.(1) $Z^* = -2.71$ <-1.645 fall in RR , Reject $H_0$

      數據顯示該診所女嬰的平均體重確實顯著低於 5.86 公斤

  (2) P-Value = P(Z<-2.71),查表 
              = 0.0034 <  $\alpha = 0.05$  &nbsp; , Reject  $H_0$
