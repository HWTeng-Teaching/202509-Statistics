__Ans:__

(1)  
right-side test.  

1.$H_0：\sigma^2=15$  

  $H_1：\sigma^2>15$  

$\alpha=0.05$  
$X^2_{STAT}=\frac{(n-1)S^2}{\sigma^2} \sim X^2_{(n-1)}$  
$X^{2*}=\frac{(25-1)21.4}{15}=34.24$  
RR={ $X^2：X^2 > 36.4151$ }  
$X^{2*}=34.24$ doesn't fall in RR.  
$\therefore$ Don't reject $H_0$.  
There is not sufficient evidence to conclude that $\sigma^2>15$.  

(2)  
CI for $\sigma^2$  
$=[\frac{(n-1)S^2}{X^2_{(n-1),\alpha/2}}，\frac{(n-1)S^2}{X^2_{(n-1),1-\alpha/2}}]$  
$=[\frac{(25-1)21.4}{X^2_{(25-1),0.05/2}}，\frac{(25-1)21.4}{X^2_{(25-1),1-0.05/2}}]$  
$=[\frac{513.6}{39.3641}，\frac{513.6}{12.4011}]$  
$\approx [13.05，41.42]$
