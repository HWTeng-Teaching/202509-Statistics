**Applying the Basics**
**7. Why Diet?**
A *USA Today* snapshot reported the results of a random sample of 500 women who were asked what reasons they might have to consider dieting, with the following results:

* **68%** Improve my health
* **51%** Look my best
* **39%** Have more energy
* **20%** Set a good example
* **17%** For my family
* **11%** Other

*Note: Respondents could select all that applied.*

**Reasons to diet**
What are the reasons you’d consider dieting?

Can we conclude that more than 60% of women considering dieting do so to “improve their health”?

**a.** How would you express the null and alternative hypotheses concerning $p$, the proportion of women in the population who would consider dieting to improve their health?
**b.** Calculate the test statistic and its *p*-value. Find the rejection region using $α=.01$.
**c.** Based on the results in part b what can you conclude about $p$?
