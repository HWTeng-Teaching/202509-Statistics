### 26. Recidivism An experimental rehabilitation technique was used on released convicts. It was shown that 79 of 121 men subjected to the technique pursued useful and crime-free lives for a 3-year period following prison release. Find a point estimate for p, the probability that a convict subjected to the rehabilitation technique will follow a crime-free existence for at least 3 years after prison release. Calculate the margin of error.
