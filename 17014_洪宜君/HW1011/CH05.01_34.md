### a.Find the expected number of years of patent life for a new drug.

### (a) Expected Value \( E[X] \)

$E[X] = \sum x p(x)$

$E[X] = 3(0.03)+4(0.05)+5(0.07)+6(0.10)+7(0.14)+8(0.20)+9(0.18)+10(0.12)+11(0.07)+12(0.03)+13(0.01)$

$E[X] = 7.9 \text{ years}$

$✅ Answer: \( E[X] = 7.9 \text{ years} \)$



### b.Find the standard deviation of x.

$\sigma^2 = \sum (x - \mu)^2 \cdot p(x) = 4.73$

$\sigma = \sqrt{\sigma^2} = \sqrt{4.73}=\approx 2.17$
