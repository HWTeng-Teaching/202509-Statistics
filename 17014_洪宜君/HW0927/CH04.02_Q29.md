## (a) Simple events ##
There are 4 leaves    
$2\times2=4\$    
$\[(M,Y),\ (M,N),\ (F,Y),\ (F,N)\]$    
Tree diagram shown in Figure 1    

## (b) Probabilities for leaves ##
| 基本事件                   | 計數 | 機率   |
| ---------------------- | -- | ---- |
| (Male, Preschool)      | 8  | 8/25 |
| (Male, No Preschool)   | 6  | 6/25 |
| (Female, Preschool)    | 9  | 9/25 |
| (Female, No Preschool) | 2  | 2/25 |


## (c) Probability student is male
$男生總共有 8 + 6 = 14 人。$   

$P(M)=\tfrac{14}{25}\$

## (d) Probability student is female and did not go to preschool   
$女生沒上幼兒園的人數 = 2。$   

$P(F,No Preschool)=\tfrac{2}{25}\$
