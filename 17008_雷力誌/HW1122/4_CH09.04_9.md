### a. If we wish to test the claim that the new tax laws help small businesses survive how would we express H0 and Ha?

目的是檢驗新的稅法是否幫助小企業提高生存率，關注的是比例下降  
虛無假設 ($H_0$): 認為比例沒有變，仍然是20%  
$$H_0: p = 0.2$$

對立假設 ($H_a$): 認為比例下降小於20%  
$$H_a: p < 0.2$$  
此題是一個左尾檢定

### b. Find the value of the test statistic based on pˆ, the sample proportion of failed small businesses.

$\hat{p} = \frac{x}{n} = \frac{30}{200} = 0.15 $  
NP與NQ都>5  
可使用比例檢驗的 $z$ 統計量公式  
$z = \frac{\hat{p} - p_0}{\sqrt{\frac{p_0(1 - p_0)}{n}}} = \frac{0.15 - 0.2}{\sqrt{\frac{0.2(0.8)}{200}}} = -1.767 \approx -1.77 $  

### c. What is the rejection region based on a significance level of a =0.05? Can you conclude that the new tax structure helps small businesses survive?

拒絕域用臨界值判斷  
臨界值:  $$z_{0.05}$$  $\approx -1.645 $  
驗統計量 $z = -1.77$ 小於 臨界值 $$z_{0.05}$$ = -1.645  
拒絕H0  
結論：在 $\alpha = 0.05$ 的顯著水準下，我們拒絕虛無假設 ($H_0: p \ge 0.20$)  
數據提供了足夠的證據支持新稅法有助於小企業生存
