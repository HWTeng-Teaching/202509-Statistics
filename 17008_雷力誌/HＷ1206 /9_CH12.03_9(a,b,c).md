### b. Find the least-squares line describing these data. Do the data indicate that there is a linear relationship between the number of miles of roadways and the year? Test using a t statistic with a =0.05.
		
<img width="598" height="527" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/a76b2352-4005-4289-8bca-63d295d381c1" />

$b = \frac{S_{xy}}{S_{xx}}= \frac{7.85}{340}=0.023$  

$a = \bar{y} - b\bar{x} = 1.04 - (0.023 \times 7.5) =0.868 $

回歸方程式 $\hat{y} = 0.868 + 0.023x$

## F-test ##
It's a right-sight test.  
$H_0：\beta=0$  
$H_1：\beta \neq 0$    
$\alpha=0.05$  
$F_{STAT}=\frac{MSR}{MSE} \sim F_{1,(n-2)}$  
$F^*=494.393$  

RR={ F：F > $F_{1,14,0.05}=4.60$  }  
$F^*=494.393$  falls in RR.  
$\therefore$ Reject $H_0$.  
There is a linear relationship between the number of miles of roadways and the year.
