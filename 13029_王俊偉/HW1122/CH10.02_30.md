It's a left-side test.  
$H_0：\mu \ge 25$  
$H_1：\mu<25$  
$\alpha=0.05$  
$t_{STAT}=\frac{\bar{X}-25}{s/\sqrt{n}} \sim t_{(21-1)}$  
$t^*=\frac{20.3-25}{5.0/\sqrt{21}}=-4.31$  
RR={t：t<-1.725}  

$t^*=-4.31$ falls in RR.  
$\therefore$ Reject $H_0$  
It's likely the true pretreatment mean is less than 25.
