### 3. $F_{STAT}=\frac{MST}{MSE} \approx F_{(k-1),(n-k)}$<br>

|Source|SS|df|MS|F|

#### MST=(SST/(k-1))<br>
=(642/(3-1)=320.94<br>
#### MSE=(SSE/(n-k))<br>
=(499)/(11-3)=62.33<br>
#### CM= $\frac{G^2}{n}$<br>
= $\frac{705,600}{11}=64,145$<br>
#### Total SS= $\displaystyle\sum x^2_{ij}-CM$<br>
= $73^2+83^2+...+87^2-64,145=65,286-64,145$<br>
=1,141<br>
#### SST= $\displaystyle\sum \frac{T^2_{i}}{n_{i}}-CM$<br>
= $\frac{380^2}{5}+\frac{199^2}{3}+\frac{261^2}{3}-64,145$<br>
= 28,880+13,200+22,707-64,145=642<br>
#### SSE=Total SS-SST<br>
=1,141-642=499<br>
