### 1. $H_0: \mu_1=\mu_2=\mu_3$ vs. $H_a$=at least one mean is different.<br>
### 2. $\alpha=.05$<br>
### 3. $F_{STAT}=\frac{MST}{MSE} \approx F_{(k-1),(n-k)}$<br>

|Source|SS|df|MS|F|
|-|-|-|-|-|
|Treatment|SST=14.5071|(k-1)=2|MST=7.2536|=6.4609|
|Error|SSE=12.35|(n-k)=11|MSE=1.1227||
|Total|Total SS=26.8571|(n-1)=13|

#### MST=(SST/(k-1))<br>
=(14.5071/(3-1)=7.2536<br>
#### MSE=(SSE/(n-k))<br>
=(12.35)/(14-3)=1.1227<br>
#### CM= $\frac{G^2}{n}$<br>
= $\frac{1444}{14}=103.1429$<br>
#### Total SS= $\displaystyle\sum x^2_{ij}-CM$<br>
= $3^2+2^2+...+1^2-103.1429=130-103.1429$<br>
=26.8571<br>
#### SST= $\displaystyle\sum \frac{T^2_{i}}{n_{i}}-CM$<br>
= $\frac{14^2}{5}+\frac{19^2}{5}+\frac{5^2}{4}-103.1429$<br>
= 117.65-103.1429=14.5071<br>
#### SSE=Total SS-SST<br>
=26.8571-14.5071=12.35<br>
