# Answer:<br>
## a.<br>
n=50<br>
$\overline{x}=8.0$<br>
95%=100(1-a)% CI for $\mu$ => a=.05<br>
margin of error($n \geq 30$)=95% margin of error= $\pm 1.96 \frac{s}{\sqrt{n}}=\pm 1.96 \frac{.3}{\sqrt{50}}=$<br>
$\pm .0832$<br>
=> $8.0 \pm .0832 => 7.9168 < \mu < 8.0832$ <br>

## b.<br>
n=50<br>
$\overline{x}=67.3$<br>
99%=100(1-a)% CI for $\mu$ => a=.01<br>
margin of error($n \geq 30$)= 99% margin of error = $\pm 2.33 \frac{s}{\sqrt{n}}=\pm 2.33 \frac{.64}{\sqrt{50}}=$<br>
$\pm .2109$<br>
=> $67.3 \pm .2109 => 67.0891 < \mu < 67.5109$ <br>
