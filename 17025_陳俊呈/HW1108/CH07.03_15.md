# Answer:<br>
Sampling Distribution approximated by the Central Limit Theorem. **The normalized sample mean has a distribution approximately as a standard normal distribution**.<br>
n=49<br>
$E(\overline{X}_n)=\mu=53$<br>
$\sigma=21$<br>
Sample standard deviation = $\frac{\sigma}{\sqrt{n}}=\frac{21}{\sqrt{49}}=3$
