If random samples of n observations are drawn from a nonnormal population with finite mean $\mu$ and standard deviation $\sigma$, then, when n is large($n \geq 30$), the sampling distribution of the sample mean $\overline{x}$ is approximately **normally distributed**, with mean $\mu$ and standard deviation $\frac{\sigma}{\sqrt{n}}$.<br>
