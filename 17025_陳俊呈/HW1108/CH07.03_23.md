## a. Find $P(1100 \leq \overline{X} \leq 1110)$<br>
$z=\frac{1110-1110}{80\sqrt{400}}=0$<br>$P(z=0)=.5000$<br>
$z=\frac{1100-1110}{80\sqrt{400}}=-2.5$<br>$P(z=-2.5)=.0062$<br>
$P(.0062 \leq z \leq .5000)=.4938$
