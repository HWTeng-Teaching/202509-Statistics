# Answer:<br>
## a.<br>
When the sample population ($\hat{p}$) is approximately symmetric, the distribution becomes approximately normal for relatively small values of n.<br>
When the sample population is skewed, the sample size must be at least 30 before the sampling distribution of $\overline{x}$ becomes approximately normal.<br>
The mean of the sampling distribution of $\hat{p}$ is p(The probability of success on a single trial), which is .47.<br>
The standard deviation of $\hat{p}$ is $\sqrt{\frac{pq}{n}}$, which is $\sqrt{\frac{(.47)(.53)}{100}}=.0499$<br>

## b.<br>
Find $P(\hat{p}<.45)$<br>
$P(\hat{p}<.45)=P(\frac{\hat{p}-.47}{.0499}<\frac{.45-.47}{.0499})$<br>
$\approx P(Z>-.4008)=.3446$<br>

## c.<br>
Find $P(.42 \leq \hat{p} \leq .45)$<br>
$P(\hat{p} \geq .42)=P(\frac{\hat{p}-.47}{.0499}<\frac{.42-.47}{.0499})$<br>
$\approx P(Z>-1.002)=.1587$<br>
