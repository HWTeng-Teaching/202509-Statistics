# Answer:<br>
## SOP:<br>
$p=p_0=.6$<br>
$q_0=1-p_0=.4$<br>
n=500<br>
$\hat{p}=.68$<br>
### 1. $H_0: p=p_0, H_a: p > p_0$<br>
### 2. <br>
$\alpha=.01, z \approx 2.33$<br>
### 3. $Z_stat=\frac{\hat{p}-p_0}{\sqrt{\frac{p_0 q_0}{n}}}$<br>
### 4. $Z*=\frac{.68-.6}{\sqrt{\frac{(.6)(.4)}{500}}} \approx 3.6515$
### 5. To find (a) or (b)<br>
(a) RR{ $z: z>z_a$ }={ $z: z>2.33$ }<br>
(b) The p-value=P(Z>Z*) => P(z>3.6515)=1-.9999 $\approx$ 0<br>
### 6.<br>
(a) Z* does fall into the RR, we reject $H_0$.<br>
(b) p-value < $\alpha$, we reject $H_0$.<br>
