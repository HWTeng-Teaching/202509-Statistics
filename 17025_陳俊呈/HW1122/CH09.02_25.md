$\mu=.8$<br>
$\overline{x}$= 79.7%<br>
n=100($\geq 30$)<br>
s=.8%<br>
### 1. $H_0=.8, H_a \neq .8$<br>
### 2. <br>
$\alpha=.05, z \approx 1.64$<br>
$\alpha/2=.025, z \approx 1.96$<br>
### 3. $Z_stat=\frac{\overline{x}-\mu}{s/\sqrt{n}}$<br>
### 4. $Z*=\frac{.797-.8}{.008/\sqrt{100}} \approx -3.75$
### 5. To find (a) or (b)<br>
(a) RR{ $z: z<-z_a/2, z>z_a/2$ }={ $z: z<-1.96, z>1.96$ }<br>
(b) The p-value=2P(Z>|Z*|) => 2P(z>|-3.75|) $\approx$ 0<br>
### 6.<br>
(a) Z* does fall into the RR, we reject $H_0$.<br>
(b) p-value < $\alpha$, we reject $H_0$.<br>
