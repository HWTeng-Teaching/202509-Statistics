### 1. $H_0: p=p_0, H_a: p < p_0$<br>
### 2. <br>
$\alpha=.05, z \approx 1.64$<br>
### 3. $Z_stat=\frac{\hat{p}-p_0}{\sqrt{\frac{p_0 q_0}{n}}}$<br>
### 4. $Z*=\frac{.15-.2}{\sqrt{\frac{(.2)(.8)}{200}}} \approx -1.7678$<br>
### 5. To find (a) or (b)<br>
(a) RR{ $z: z<-z_a$ }={ $z: z<-1.64$ }<br>
(b) The p-value=P(Z<Z*) => P(z<-1.7678) $\approx$ .0392<br>
### 6.<br>
(a) Z* does fall into the RR, we reject $H_0$.<br>
(b) p-value < $\alpha$, we reject $H_0$.<br>
