## a.<br>

### SOP:<br>
#### 1. $H_0: \mu \geq \mu_0(25), H_a: \mu < \mu_0(25)$<br>
#### 2. <br>
95%=100(1-a)% CI for $\mu$ => $\alpha=.05$<br>
n=21<br>
df=n-1=20<br>
$t_.05=1.725$<br>
#### 3. $tSTAT=\frac{\overline{X}-\mu_0}{s/\sqrt{n}}$<br>
#### 4. $t*=\frac{20.3-25}{5/\sqrt{21}})=-4.3076$<br>
#### 5. To find RR<br>
RR{ $t: t<-t \alpha$ }<br>
#### 6.<br>
t* does fall into the RR, we reject $H_0$.<br>
The evidence is sufficient to conclude that the true pretreatment mean is less than 25.<br>
