在 15–17 題的每一個情境中，請描述「樣本平均數的抽樣分配」的大致形狀，並求其平均數與標準誤。

15. 從一個母體中隨機抽取樣本，樣本大小為 n=49；此母體的平均數為 μ=53，母體標準差為 σ=21

---------------
### 1. Shape of the Sampling Distribution
Since  
n = 49 $\ge$ 30  
by the Central Limit Theorem, the sampling distribution of the sample mean $\bar{X}$ is **approximately normal**.

---

### 2. Mean of the Sampling Distribution
$\mu_{\bar{X}} = \mu = 53$

---

### 3. Standard Error (SE)
$\sigma_{\bar{X}} = \frac{\sigma}{\sqrt{n}}$
$= \frac{21}{\sqrt{49}}$
$= \frac{21}{7}$
= 3

---

## Conclusion
- Shape: **Approximately Normal**
- Mean: **53**
- Standard Error: **3**
