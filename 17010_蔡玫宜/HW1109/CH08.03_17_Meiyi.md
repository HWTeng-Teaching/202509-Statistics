17.根據一個隨機樣本（來自二項實驗）中 500 次試驗，其中成功次數為 27，求 𝑝 的 95% 信賴區間。

## Problem 17 — 95% CI for p

### Given:
- n = 500  
- x = 27  
- Confidence level = 95%

### 1. Sample proportion

$\hat{p} = \frac{27}{500} = 0.054$

### 2. z-value for 95% CI
$z = 1.96$

### 3. Standard error (SE)

$SE = \sqrt{\frac{\hat{p}(1-\hat{p})}{n}}= \sqrt{\frac{0.054(1-0.054)}{500}}= 0.0101$

### 4. Margin of error (ME)

$ME = 1.96 \times 0.0101 = 0.0198$

### 5. Confidence interval

$CI = 0.054 \pm 0.0198$

$\boxed{(0.0342, 0.0738)}$

### Interpretation
We are 95% confident that the true population proportion \(p\) lies between **0.0342** and **0.0738**.
