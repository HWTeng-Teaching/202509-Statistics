**Data:** n = 7: 1.4, 3.6, 1.7, 2.0, 3.3, 2.8, 2.9

**Tasks:**
1. Calculate the sample variance, s²
2. Construct a 95% confidence interval for the population variance, σ²
3. Test the hypothesis: H₀: σ² = 0.8 versus Hₐ: σ² ≠ 0.8 (α = 0.05)

---

## Solution

### (1) Calculate Sample Variance s²

**Step 1: Create calculation table**

| No | x | x² |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| 1 | 1.4 | 1.96 |
| 2 | 1.7 | 2.89 |
| 3 | 2.0 | 4.00 |
| 4 | 2.8 | 7.84 |
| 5 | 2.9 | 8.41 |
| 6 | 3.3 | 10.89 |
| 7 | 3.6 | 12.96 |
| **sum** | **17.7** | **48.95** |

**Step 2: Apply the formula**

$$s^2 = \frac{\sum x_i^2 - \frac{(\sum x_i)^2}{n}}{n-1} = \frac{48.95 - \frac{17.7^2}{7}}{7-1} = \frac{48.95 - 44.757}{6} \approx 0.70$$

**Result: s² ≈ 0.70**

---

### (2) 95% Confidence Interval for σ²

**Formula:**

$$\left[\frac{(n-1)s^2}{\chi^2_{(n-1), \alpha/2}}, \frac{(n-1)s^2}{\chi^2_{(n-1), 1-\alpha/2}}\right]$$

**Given:**
- α = 1 - 0.95 = 0.05
- df = n - 1 = 6
- χ²₍₆,₀.₀₂₅₎ = 14.4494
- χ²₍₆,₀.₉₇₅₎ = 1.237347

**Calculation:**

$$\left[\frac{(7-1) \times 0.7}{14.4494}, \frac{(7-1) \times 0.7}{1.237347}\right] = \left[\frac{4.2}{14.4494}, \frac{4.2}{1.237347}\right] \approx [0.29, 3.39]$$

**Result: 95% CI for σ² ≈ [0.29, 3.39]**

---

### (3) Hypothesis Testing

**Hypotheses:**
- H₀: σ² = 0.8
- H₁: σ² ≠ 0.8
- α = 0.05 (two-sided test)

**Test Statistic:**

$$\chi^2_{STAT} = \frac{(n-1)s^2}{\sigma_0^2} \sim \chi^2_{(n-1)}$$

$$\chi^{2*} = \frac{(7-1) \times 0.7}{0.8} = \frac{4.2}{0.8} = 5.25$$

**Rejection Region:**

$$RR = \{\chi^2 : \chi^2 < 1.237347 \text{ or } \chi^2 > 14.4494\}$$

**Decision:**

Since χ²* = 5.25 does not fall in the rejection region (1.237347 < 5.25 < 14.4494),

**∴ We do not reject H₀**

**Conclusion:** There is not sufficient evidence to conclude that the population variance is different from 0.8 at the 0.05 significance level.
