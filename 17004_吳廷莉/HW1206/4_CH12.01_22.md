### c. Calculate the regression line for predicting height based on a person’s armspan. Does the value of the slope b confirm your conclusions in part b?

<img width="1268" height="248" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/91b6c2a9-eaed-42ae-bd3b-b769e13a48a7" />
<img width="984" height="698" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/d2f64bbb-1ac8-4383-9a12-6477fd4bdd60" />

$b = \frac{S_{xy}}{S_{xx}}= \frac{468}{568}=0.824$  

$a = \bar{y} - b\bar{x} = 166.375 - (0.824 \times 166) =29.6 $

回歸方程式 $\hat{y} = 29.6 + 0.824x$

### d. If a person has an armspan of 157 centimeters, what would you predict the person’s height to be? 

題目問：如果某人的臂展是 157 cm，預測他的身高是多少？  
代入回歸方程式：  
$$\hat{y} = 29.6 + 0.824(157) = 158.96 $$
