## Answer: 

此提要求根據提供的樣本數據，建構 $95\%$ 信賴區間來估計單片包裝起司的平均價格  
<img width="302" height="350" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/e2f61535-8966-408d-9955-4bd8435d5b91" />


由於總體標準差 ($\sigma$) 未知，且樣本大小 $n=13 < 30$ (小樣本)  
使用T分佈來建構信賴區間  
自由度 = n-1 = 13-1 = 12

<img width="598" height="446" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/258a5757-b1e2-4328-8ad0-c0a1df6cb512" />

查T表得知 $t_{0.025, 12} \approx \mathbf{2.179}$
<img width="973" height="581" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/7ef456b1-546f-41c7-9b4a-a8669a334f1c" />


$\text{信賴區間} = \bar{x} \pm t_{\alpha/2, df} \left( \frac{s}{\sqrt{n}} \right)$

$\text{信賴區間} = 24.7769 \pm 2.179 \times  \frac{3.341}{\sqrt{13}} =  ( 22.76 , 26.80 ) $  
95%的信心水準下，單片獨立包裝起司的平均價格介於 22.76 至 26.80 之間
