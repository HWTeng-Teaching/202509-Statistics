## Answer 7:
## 7. 檢驗 $\mathbf{H_0: \sigma^2 = 21.4}$ vs. $\mathbf{H_a: \sigma^2 > 15}$

此題為右尾檢定  
講義公式：
<img width="627" height="170" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/784913d8-5dac-4354-ba4d-ea871c5e3fa3" />

$$\chi^2 = \frac{(n-1)s^2}{\sigma^2}$$$$\chi^2 = \frac{(24)(21.4)}{15} = \frac{513.6}{15} \approx \mathbf{34.24}$$

臨界值: 查 $\chi^2$ 表，右尾面積為 $\alpha=0.05$，自由度 $df=24$  
$$\chi^2_{\text{crit}} = \mathbf{36.415}$$  
<img width="742" height="634" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/33595c11-4847-41d2-8142-1b6b365e8d4d" />

檢驗統計量 $\chi^2 = 34.24$ 小於臨界值 $36.415$  
不在拒絕域，故不拒絕H0

## Answer 9:
## 7. 檢驗 $\mathbf{H_0: \sigma^2 = 0.8}$ vs. $\mathbf{H_a: \sigma^2 \neq 0.8}$

<img width="293" height="217" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/3660b0ff-1007-4ebe-a198-a0d2f0b392aa" />


此題為雙尾檢定  

$$\chi^2 = \frac{(n-1)s^2}{\sigma^2}$$$$\chi^2 = \frac{(6)(0.69905)}{0.8} = \frac{4.1943}{0.8} \approx \mathbf{5.243}$$
臨界值: 查 $\chi^2$ 表，右尾面積為 $\alpha=0.025$，自由度 $df=6$  
查表為14.4494

臨界值: 查 $\chi^2$ 表，左尾面積為 $\alpha=0.975$，自由度 $df=6$  
查表為1.237347
