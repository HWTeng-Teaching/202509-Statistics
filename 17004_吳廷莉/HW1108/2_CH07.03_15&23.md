## Answer:
N=400；σ=80；μ=1110

<img width="1580" height="644" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/3183e8ae-5fc7-48e7-94e2-2498e3d83126" />

計算Z標準差 來查表取得機率
### a. The average is between 1100 and 1110.
1100<=X<=1110 

$Z(X=1110)=\frac{\overline{X}_n - \mu}{\sigma / \sqrt{n}}=\frac{1110-1110}{80 / \sqrt{400}}=0$

查表Z(0)=0.5

$Z(X=1100)=\frac{1100-1110}{80 / \sqrt{400}}=\frac{-10}{80/20}=\frac{-10}{4}=-2.5$

查表Z(-2.5)=0.0062

P(−2.5<Z<0)=P(Z<0)−P(Z<−2.5)=0.5−0.0062=0.4938

P(1100< $\bar{X}$ <1110)=0.4938

### b. The average is greater than 1120.

X>1120

$Z(X=1120)=\frac{\overline{X}_n - \mu}{\sigma / \sqrt{n}}=\frac{1120-1110}{80 / \sqrt{400}}=2.5$

查表Z(2.5)=0.9938

P($\bar{X}$ >1120)=

P(Z>2.5)=1−P(Z<2.5)=1-0.9938=0.0062

### c. The average is less than 900.

X<900

$Z(X=900)=\frac{\overline{X}_n - \mu}{\sigma / \sqrt{n}}=\frac{900-1110}{80 / \sqrt{400}}=-52.5$

查表Z(-52.5)=0

P($\bar{X}$ <900)= P(Z<-52.5) = 0
