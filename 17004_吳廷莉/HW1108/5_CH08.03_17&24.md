## Answer:

weight mean = 8 ;  weight s = 0.3
length mean = 67.3 ;  length s = 0.64

 
### a. Find a 95% confidence interval for the average weight of all six-month-old boys. 

MOE = $\pm 1.96 * \dfrac{s}{\sqrt{n}} = \pm 1.96 * \dfrac{0.3}{\sqrt{50}} = \pm 1.96 * 0.0424 = \pm 0.0832$

Interpret the interval = [ meau $\pm$ 0.0832 ] = [ 8 $\pm$ 0.0832 ] = [ 7.9168 , 8.0832 ]

### ab. Find a 99% confidence interval for the average length of all six-month-old boys.

MOE = $\pm 2.57 * \dfrac{s}{\sqrt{n}} = \pm 2.57 * \dfrac{0.64}{\sqrt{50}} = \pm 2.57 * 0.0905 = \pm 0.2326$

Interpret the interval = [ meau $\pm$ 0.2326 ] = [ 67.3 $\pm$ 0.2326 ] = [ 67.0674 , 67.5326 ]
