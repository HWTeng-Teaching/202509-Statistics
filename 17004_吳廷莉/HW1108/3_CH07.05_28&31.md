### probability that the sample proportion of players born outside the United States exceeds 80%
X=P=0.7  ； q=1-0.7=0.3

σ =pq=0.21

$Z(x=0.8)=\frac{\hat{p} - p}{ \sqrt{pq/n}}=\frac{0.8-0.7}{\sqrt{0.21/50}}=1.543$
Z(1.54)=0.9382

X>0.8 -> P(Z>1.54)

P(Z>1.54)=1−P(Z<1.54)=1-0.9382=0.0618

## Answer:
### a. Does the distribution of pˆ, the sample proportion of Americans who associate “recycling” with Earth Day, have an approximate normal distribution? If so, what is its mean and standard deviation?

mean=p=0.47

standard deviation= $\sqrt{\frac{pq}{n}} = \sqrt{\frac{0.47*0.53}{100}}=0.0499$

### b. What is the probability that the sample proportion pˆ is less than 0.45?

p(pˆ<0.45)

$Z(x=0.45)=\frac{\hat{p} - p}{ \sqrt{pq/n}}=\frac{0.45-0.47}{0.0499}=-0.4$

查表Z(-0.4)=0.3446

p(z<0.45)=0.3446

### c. What is the probability that pˆ lies in the interval .42 to .45?

p(0.42<pˆ<0.45)

$Z(x=0.45)=\frac{\hat{p} - p}{ \sqrt{pq/n}}=\frac{0.45-0.47}{0.0499}=-0.4$

查表Z(-0.4)=0.3446

$Z(x=0.42)=\frac{\hat{p} - p}{ \sqrt{pq/n}}=\frac{0.42-0.47}{0.0499}=-1$

查表Z(-1)=0.1587

p(0.42<pˆ<0.45)=p(Z=-1)-p(Z=-0.4)=0.3446-0.1587=0.1859

### d. What might you conclude about p if the observed sample proportion were less than 0.30?

Z(x=0.3)=\frac{\hat{p} - p}{ \sqrt{pq/n}}=\frac{0.3-0.47}{0.0499}=-3.41$
查表Z(-3.41)=0.0003
觀察到x=p=0.3的機率為0.0003 如此低的機率顯示在p=0.47 假設下幾乎不可能
