利用中央極限定理 轉化為Z值 查詢機率

### probability that the sample proportion of players born outside the United States exceeds 80%
X=P=0.7  ； q=1-0.7=0.3

σ =pq=0.21

$Z(x=0.8)=\frac{\hat{p} - p}{ \sqrt{\pq/n}}=\frac{0.8-0.7}{\sqrt{0.21/50}}=1.543$
Z(1.54)=0.9382

X>0.8 -> P(Z>1.54)

P(Z>1.54)=1−P(Z<1.54)=1-0.9382=0.0618
