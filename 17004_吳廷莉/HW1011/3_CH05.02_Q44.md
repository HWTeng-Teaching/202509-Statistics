## Answer:

### a. What is the probability that more than 6 preferred McDonald’s?
<img width="920" height="331" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/e72d0f99-0571-4111-b7b5-6702018b8e82" />

n=10 , p=0.5 , x=preferred McDonald’s frequency

p(x>6)= p(x=10) - p(x=6) = 1 - 0.828 = 0.172

### b. What is the probability that between 4 and 6 (inclusive) preferred McDonald’s?

p(x<=6)=0.828

p(x<=3)= 0.172 

p(4<=x<=6) = 0.828 - 0.172 = 0.656

### c. What is the probability that between 4 and 6 (inclusive) preferred Burger King?

p=0.5 表示preferred McDonald’s/preferred Burger King各一半

所以X=設定為preferred Burger參數與preferred McDonald’s一樣

答案同b

p(4<=x<=6) = 0.828 - 0.172 = 0.656
