### 統計學解題報告 (Statistics Problem Solution)

**題目摘要:** 根據 USA Today 的調查，500 位女性中有 68% 表示節食是為了「改善健康」。在顯著水準 $\alpha = .01$ 下，檢定母體比例是否超過 60%。

---

#### a. 建立假設 (Hypotheses)
我們想檢定比例是否 **大於 (more than)** 0.60，故為右尾檢定。 Let $p$ be the proportion of women in the population who would consider dieting to improve their health.

- **Null Hypothesis ($H_0$):** $p \leq 0.60$ (or $p = 0.60$)
- **Alternative Hypothesis ($H_a$):** $p > 0.60$

---

#### b. 計算統計量與拒絕域 (Calculations)

**已知數據:**
- $n = 500$
- $\hat{p} = 0.68$
- $p_0 = 0.60$
- $\alpha = 0.01$

**1. 檢定統計量 (Test Statistic $z$):**
公式: $z = \frac{\hat{p} - p_0}{\sqrt{\frac{p_0(1-p_0)}{n}}}$

$$
z = \frac{0.68 - 0.60}{\sqrt{\frac{0.60(1-0.60)}{500}}} 
= \frac{0.08}{\sqrt{\frac{0.24}{500}}} 
= \frac{0.08}{0.0219} 
\approx \mathbf{3.65}
$$

**2. 拒絕域 (Rejection Region):**
對於單尾 (右尾) 檢定，$\alpha = 0.01$。
查標準常態分配表 (Z-table)，臨界值 $z_{0.01} \approx 2.33$。
- **拒絕域:** Reject $H_0$ if $z > 2.33$.

**3. P-value:**
$P(Z > 3.65) \approx 0.00013$

---

#### c. 結論 (Conclusion)

比較統計量與臨界值：
$$3.65 > 2.33$$

**結果:** Reject the null hypothesis ($H_0$).

**解釋:** Based on the results, there is sufficient evidence at the 0.01 level of significance to conclude that **more than 60%** of women considering dieting do so to improve their health.
