### 統計學解題報告: New Tax Laws (Hypothesis Test)

**題目摘要:** 已知小企業第一年失敗率基準為 20%。隨機抽樣 200 家新企業，發現 30 家失敗。在 $\alpha = .05$ 下，檢定新稅法是否有效降低了失敗率。

---

#### a. 建立假設 (Hypotheses)
我們希望檢定失敗率 ($p$) 是否**低於** 0.20 (幫助生存)。
Let $p$ be the proportion of small businesses that fail in their first year.

- **Null Hypothesis ($H_0$):** $p \geq 0.20$ (The laws do not help)
- **Alternative Hypothesis ($H_a$):** $p < 0.20$ (The laws help / reduce failure rate)

---

#### b. 計算檢定統計量 (Test Statistic)

**數據:**
- $n = 200$
- Sample proportion $\hat{p} = \frac{30}{200} = 0.15$
- Hypothesized proportion $p_0 = 0.20$

$$
z = \frac{\hat{p} - p_0}{\sqrt{\frac{p_0(1-p_0)}{n}}} 
= \frac{0.15 - 0.20}{\sqrt{\frac{0.20(0.80)}{200}}} 
= \frac{-0.05}{\sqrt{0.0008}} 
\approx \mathbf{-1.77}
$$

---

#### c. 拒絕域與結論 (Rejection Region & Conclusion)
Using $\alpha = 0.05$ for a left-tailed test:
- **Critical Value:** $z_{0.05} = -1.645$
- **Rejection Region:** Reject $H_0$ if $z < -1.645$.

**Decision:**
Since $-1.77 < -1.645$, the test statistic falls in the rejection region. We **reject** $H_0$.

**Conclusion:**
There is sufficient evidence at the $\alpha = .05$ level to conclude that the new tax structure helps small businesses survive (the failure rate is significantly less than 20%).

---

#### d. P-value Calculation
The $p$-value is the probability of obtaining a sample proportion as low as 0.15 if the true proportion were 0.20.

$$P(Z < -1.77) \approx \mathbf{0.0384}$$

**Does the conclusion change?**
No. Since the $p$-value ($0.0384$) is less than $\alpha$ ($0.05$), the decision is still to reject the null hypothesis.
