## 25. Antibiotics
### 已知參數
- $n = 100$
- $\bar{x} = 1020$
- $s = 90$

### 計算過程
1. **點估計量 (Point Estimate)**:
   $$\text{Point Estimate} = \bar{x} = 1020$$
2. **誤差範圍 (Margin of Error, 假設 95% CI)**:
   $$\text{MOE} = 1.96 \times \frac{s}{\sqrt{n}} = 1.96 \times \frac{90}{10} = 17.64$$

## 26. Recidivism
### 已知參數
- $n = 121$
- $x = 79$

### 計算過程
1. **點估計量 ($\hat{p}$)**:
   $$\hat{p} = \frac{79}{121} \approx 0.6529$$
2. **誤差範圍 (Margin of Error, 假設 95% CI)**:
   $$\text{MOE} = 1.96 \times \sqrt{\frac{\hat{p}(1-\hat{p})}{n}}$$
   $$\text{MOE} = 1.96 \times \sqrt{\frac{0.6529(0.3471)}{121}} \approx 0.0848$$
