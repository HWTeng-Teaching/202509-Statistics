以95%的信賴去間水準下計算

mean=p=79/121=0.6529

q=1-p=0.3471

MOE = $\pm$ 1.96*SE

$= \pm 1.96  * \sqrt{\frac{pq}{n}} = \pm \sqrt{\frac{0.6529 * 0.3471}{121}}= \pm 1.96*0.043277 $

= $$\pm$$ 0.0848
