## F-test ##
It's a right-sight test.  
$H_0：\beta=0$  
$H_1：\beta \neq 0$    
$\alpha=0.05$  
$F_{STAT}=\frac{MSR}{MSE} \sim F_{1,(n-2)}$  
$F^*=24$  

RR={ F：F > $F_{1,6,0.05}=5.99$  }  
$F^*=24$  falls in RR.  
$\therefore$ Reject $H_0$.  
At the 5% significance level, the model is useful in predicting $y$.  

## Caculate b and its standard error ##
$b=\frac{S_{xy}}{S_{xx}}=\frac{8}{4}=2$  
$SE(b)=\sqrt{\frac{MSE}{S_{xx}}}=\sqrt{\frac{0.667}{4}} \approx 0.41$  

## t-test ##
It's a two-sight test.  
$H_0：\beta=0$  
$H_1：\beta \neq 0$    
$\alpha=0.05$  
$t_{STAT}=\frac{b-0}{\sqrt{\frac{MSE}{S_{xx}}}} \sim t_{n-2}$  
$t^* \approx 4.88$  

RR={ t：|t| > $t_{6,0.05}=2.447$  }  
$t^*=4.88$  falls in RR.  
$\therefore$ Reject $H_0$.
