It's a right-sight test.  
$H_0：\mu_1=\mu_2=\mu_3$  
$H_1：$ at least one mean is different    
$\alpha=0.05$  
<img width="539" height="430" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/d971b4a9-bc15-40fc-b378-4a6e23e40e5d" />

$F_{STAT}=\frac{MST}{MSE} \sim F_{(k-1),(n-k)}$  
$F^*=5.15$  

RR={ F：F > $F_{2,8,0.05}=4.46$  }  
$F^*=5.15$  falls in RR.  

$p-value:P(F^*|H_0)=P(F_{2,8}>5.15)<0.05$  
$\because$ $p-value<\alpha=0.05$  
$\therefore$ Reject $H_0$.  
It's likely at least one mean is different.
