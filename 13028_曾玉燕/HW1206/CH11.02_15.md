It's a right-sight test.  
$H_0：\mu_1=\mu_2=\mu_3=\mu_4$  
$H_1：$ at least one mean is different    
$\alpha=0.05$  
$F_{STAT}=\frac{MST}{MSE} \sim F_{(k-1),(n-k)}$  
$F^*=26.44$  

RR={ F：F > $F_{3,16,0.05}=3.24$  }  
$F^*=26.44$  falls in RR.  

$p-value:P(F^*|H_0)=P(F_{3,16}>26.44)<0.005$  
$\because$ $p-value<\alpha=0.05$  
$\therefore$ Reject $H_0$.  
It's likely at least one mean is different. sufficient evidence to indicate that the average price per 1000 board meters of framing lumber differs among the four states
