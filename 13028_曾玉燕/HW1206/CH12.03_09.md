## the least-squares line ##
$b=\frac{S_{xy}}{S_{xx}}=\frac{7.85}{340} \approx 0.023$  
$a=\bar{y}-b\bar{x}=1.04-0.023 \times 7.5=1$  
the least-squares line: $\hat{y}=1+0.023x$  

## t-test ##
It's a two-sight test.  
$H_0：\rho=0$  
$H_1：\rho \neq 0$    
$\alpha=0.05$  
$t_{STAT}=r \sqrt{\frac{n-2}{(1-r^2)}} \sim t_{n-2}$  
$r=\frac{S_{xy}}{\sqrt{S_{xx}S_{yy}}}=\frac{7.85}{\sqrt{340 \times 0.19}} \approx 0.99$  
$t^* \approx 22.23$  

RR={ t：|t| > $t_{14,0.05}=2.145$  }  
$t^*=22.23$  falls in RR.  
$\therefore$ Reject $H_0$.  
There is a linear relationship between the number of miles of roadways and the year.
