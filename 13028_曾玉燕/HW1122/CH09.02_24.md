It's a right-side test.  
$H_0 : \mu < 5.86$  
$H_1 : \mu \ge 5.86$  
$\alpha=0.05$  
$Z_{STAT}=\frac{\bar{X}-\mu}{S/\sqrt{n}}=\frac{5.56-5.86}{0.7/\sqrt{40}} \approx -2.71$  
RR={Z：Z>1.645}  
$Z^*=-2.71$ doesn't fall in RR.  
$\therefore$ Don't reject $H_0$
