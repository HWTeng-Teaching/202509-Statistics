$p=1/1000=0.001$  
$Binomial(n=50000,p=0.001) ≈ N(μ=np,σ^2=npq)$  
$\mu=np=50000 \times 0.001=50$  
$\sigma= \sqrt{npq}=\sqrt{50000 \times 0.001 \times (1-0.001)}=7.07$  
$z= \frac{x-0.5-\mu}{\sigma}=\frac{60-0.5-50}{7.07} =1.34$
