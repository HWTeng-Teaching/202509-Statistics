$Binomial(n=100,p=0.51) ≈ N(μ=np,σ^2=npq)$  
$\mu=100 \times 0.51=51$  
$\sigma= \sqrt{npq}=\sqrt{100 \times 0.51 \times (1-0.51)}=5$  
$z= \frac{x \pm 0.5-\mu}{\sigma}=\frac{60-0.5-51}{5}=1.7$
