It would be very unusual to observe exactly one plant with red petals,
because the probability of this outcome is extremely small under the assumed binomial model.
However, this result is not impossible — rare events can still occur by chance.
If such an outcome were actually observed, it might suggest that
the true probability of red petals differs from the expected value.
