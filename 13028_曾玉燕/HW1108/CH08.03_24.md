A:  
$\mu=\bar{x}=8.0$  
$n=50 \ge 30$  
95% confidence interval (z = 1.96)  
confidence interval $=\bar{x} \pm Z_{\alpha/2} \frac{s}{\sqrt{n}}=8.0 \pm 1.96 \frac{0.3}{\sqrt{50}}=8.0 \pm 0.083=[7.917,8.083]$
