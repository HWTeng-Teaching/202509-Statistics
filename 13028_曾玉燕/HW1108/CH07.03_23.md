A:  
$n=400 \ge 30$ meets the condition of CLT.  
Thus, if the population is a normal distribution, the shape of the sampling distribution must be a standard normal distribution.  
If not, the shape of the sampling distribution approximates a standard normal distribution. 

formula: $\frac{\bar{X}_n-\mu}{\sigma / \sqrt{n}}$  

$P(1100 \le \bar{X}_{400} \le 1110)$  

$=P(\frac{1100-1110}{80/\sqrt{400}}\le \frac{\bar{X}_{400}-1110}{80/\sqrt{400}} \le \frac{1110-1110}{80/\sqrt{400}})$  

$=P(-2.5 \le Z \le 0)$
