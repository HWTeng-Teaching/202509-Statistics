A:  
Suppose $X \sim Binomial(n,p)$ ，denote $\hat{p}= \frac{X}{n}$，then $\frac{\hat{p}-p}{\sqrt{\frac{pq}{n}}} \xrightarrow{d} N(0,1)$  

$np=50 \times 0.7=35 >30$ and $nq=50 \times 0.3=15 >30$  

$P(\hat{p} > 0.8)$  
$=P(\frac{\hat{p}-0.7}{\sqrt{\frac{0.7 \times 0.3}{50}}} > \frac{0.8-0.7}{\sqrt{\frac{0.7 \times 0.3}{50}}})$  
$=P(Z > 1.54)$  
$=1-P(Z \le 1.54)$  
$=1-0.9382$  
$=0.0618$<sub>#<sub>
