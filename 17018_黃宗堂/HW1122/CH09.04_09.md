**題目摘要:** 已知小企業第一年失敗率為 20%。在隨機抽樣的 200 家新企業中，有 30 家失敗。在 $\alpha = .05$ 下，檢定新稅法是否降低了失敗率（幫助企業生存）。

---

#### a. 建立假設 (Hypotheses)
Let $p$ be the proportion of small businesses that fail in their first year. If the laws help, $p$ should decrease.

- **Null Hypothesis ($H_0$):** $p \geq 0.20$ (The laws do not reduce failure rate)
- **Alternative Hypothesis ($H_a$):** $p < 0.20$ (The laws reduce failure rate)

---

#### b. 計算檢定統計量 (Test Statistic)

**數據:**
- $n = 200$
- Sample proportion $\hat{p} = \frac{30}{200} = 0.15$
- Hypothesized proportion $p_0 = 0.20$

$$
z = \frac{\hat{p} - p_0}{\sqrt{\frac{p_0(1-p_0)}{n}}} 
= \frac{0.15 - 0.20}{\sqrt{\frac{0.20(0.80)}{200}}} 
= \frac{-0.05}{\sqrt{0.0008}} 
\approx \mathbf{-1.77}
$$

---

#### c. 拒絕域與結論 (Rejection Region & Conclusion)
Using $\alpha = 0.05$ for a left-tailed test:
- **Critical Value:** $z_{0.05} = -1.645$
- **Rejection Region:** Reject $H_0$ if $z < -1.645$

**Decision:**
Since $-1.77 < -1.645$, we **reject** $H_0$.

**Conclusion:**
There is sufficient evidence at the 0.05 significance level to conclude that the new tax structure helps small businesses survive (by reducing the failure rate).

---

#### d. P-value
The $p$-value is the probability of observing a $z$-score of -1.77 or less.

$$P(Z < -1.77) \approx \mathbf{0.0384}$$

**Does the conclusion change?**
No. Since the $p$-value ($0.0384$) is less than $\alpha$ ($0.05$), the decision remains to reject the null hypothesis.
