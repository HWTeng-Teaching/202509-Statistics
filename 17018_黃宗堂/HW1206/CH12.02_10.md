# 第 10 題：Body Mass Index (BMI) 與收入之迴歸分析

**題目數據摘要：**
* **Income ($x$):** 15, 20.5, 30, 40, 60, 75
* **BMI ($y$):** 31.2, 29.3, 27.4, 27.3, 26.8, 20.0
* **樣本數 ($n$):** 6

---

## a. 變數識別 (Variable Identification)

題目陳述為 "BMI as a function of income"（BMI 為收入的函數），這表示收入是用來預測 BMI 的。

* **獨立變數 (Independent variable, $x$):** Income (收入, 千美元)
* **應變數 (Dependent variable, $y$):** BMI (身體質量指數)

---

## b. 尋找最小平方法迴歸線 (Least-Squares Line)

我們需要找出迴歸方程式 $\hat{y} = b_0 + b_1x$。

**1. 計算基本統計量：**

* $\sum x = 15 + 20.5 + 30 + 40 + 60 + 75 = 240.5$
* $\sum y = 31.2 + 29.3 + 27.4 + 27.3 + 26.8 + 20.0 = 162.0$
* $\bar{x} = 240.5 / 6 \approx 40.0833$
* $\bar{y} = 162.0 / 6 = 27.0$

**2. 計算平方和 (Sum of Squares)：**

* **$SS_{xy}$ (交叉乘積和):**
    $$SS_{xy} = \sum xy - \frac{(\sum x)(\sum y)}{n}$$
    $$\sum xy = (15)(31.2) + (20.5)(29.3) + \dots + (75)(20.0) = 6090.65$$
    $$SS_{xy} = 6090.65 - \frac{(240.5)(162)}{6} = 6090.65 - 6493.5 = \mathbf{-402.85}$$

* **$SS_{xx}$ (X 的離差平方和):**
    $$SS_{xx} = \sum x^2 - \frac{(\sum x)^2}{n}$$
    $$\sum x^2 = 15^2 + 20.5^2 + \dots + 75^2 = 12370.25$$
    $$SS_{xx} = 12370.25 - \frac{(240.5)^2}{6} \approx 12370.25 - 9640.04 = \mathbf{2730.21}$$

**3. 計算斜率 ($b_1$) 與截距 ($b_0$)：**

* **斜率 ($b_1$):**
    $$b_1 = \frac{SS_{xy}}{SS_{xx}} = \frac{-402.85}{2730.21} \approx \mathbf{-0.1476}$$

* **截距 ($b_0$):**
    $$b_0 = \bar{y} - b_1\bar{x} = 27.0 - (-0.1476)(40.0833)$$
    $$b_0 = 27.0 - (-5.916) \approx \mathbf{32.916}$$

**結論：**
最小平方法迴歸線為：
$$\hat{y} = 32.916 - 0.148x$$

---

## c. 建構變異數分析表 (ANOVA Table)

**1. 計算各項平方和 (Sum of Squares)：**

* **總平方和 ($SST$):**
    $$SST = SS_{yy} = \sum y^2 - \frac{(\sum y)^2}{n}$$
    $$\sum y^2 = 31.2^2 + \dots + 20.0^2 = 4446.22$$
    $$SST = 4446.22 - \frac{162^2}{6} = 4446.22 - 4374 = \mathbf{72.22}$$

* **迴歸平方和 ($SSR$):**
    $$SSR = \frac{(SS_{xy})^2}{SS_{xx}} = \frac{(-402.85)^2}{2730.21} \approx \mathbf{59.44}$$
 

* **誤差平方和 ($SSE$):**
    $$SSE = SST - SSR = 72.22 - 59.44 = \mathbf{12.78}$$

**2. 計算自由度 ($df$) 與均方 ($MS$):**

* $df_{Regression} = 1$
* $df_{Error} = n - 2 = 6 - 2 = 4$
* $df_{Total} = n - 1 = 5$
* $MSR = SSR / 1 = 59.44$
* $MSE = SSE / 4 = 12.78 / 4 = 3.195$

**3. 計算 F 值:**
$$F = \frac{MSR}{MSE} = \frac{59.44}{3.195} \approx \mathbf{18.60}$$
